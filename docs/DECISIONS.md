- 2026-11-08 — render: Reveal history is CPU-authoritative (Float32 master + RG8 DataTexture upload at 20 Hz) instead of a GPU-only RT. `revealAt` samples the exact bytes the shader samples, so gameplay visibility and pixels agree with no readback; the e2e probe measures max |GPU−CPU| field error ≈ 0.0014 with 0 visibility mismatches over 1024 points. Cost: one 65k-texel fade loop + 128 KB upload per stamp tick.
- 2026-11-08 — render: Reveal noise comes from one tileable 256² RGBA8 value-noise texture (4 channels: reveal edge, band wobble, stroke breaks, grain). Cheaper than ALU fbm on mobile and mirrorable on the CPU (bilinear REPEAT, texel-centre exact). All reveal sources combine in *field* space with one shared paper-absorbency offset, so overlapping circles, trails and history merge with one coherent ragged edge.
- 2026-11-08 — render: Outline pick = **AB hybrid** (default). A (inverted hull, smoothed `hullNormal`, screen-constant width, 12 Hz boil) on characters, ghosts, props and sketch objects; B (post Sobel on a float depth texture: silhouettes + crease from view-space tangent bend angle) on everything. A alone loses environment silhouettes; B alone gives thin, uneven character lines at game distance. Toggle with `render.setOutlineMode('A'|'B'|'AB')` / harness "lines" button. B runs at half res when `quality.halfResEdges` (mobile auto, low).
- 2026-11-08 — render: Colour RT alpha carries the *ink class* (1 inked / 0.5 wet rim & FX / 0 paper). Post uses it to apply line screen only to printed ink and to draw pencil (broken, doubled) instead of ink edges on unrevealed paper. Third-party materials that write alpha 1 are treated as inked.
- 2026-11-08 — render: Panel cuts render 2–3 cameras into non-overlapping scissor rects of the one colour RT; slanted frames/gutters, speed lines and the onomatopoeia stamp are composed in the same post pass (no extra passes). Stamp lettering is baked to a channel-packed canvas texture (R ink, G fill, B burst, A shadow) so the post composes it with exact palette colours. Panel cut costs ≈3× scene draws while active (~114 draws in the harness, under the 120 budget only just — keep panel scenes lean).
- 2026-11-08 — render: Page turn captures the composited old frame into an RT, rolls a 64×64 grid around a moving cylinder in the vertex shader (front = old page, back = paper with show-through), new scene renders live underneath; the post pass casts the roll's shadow. The animation clock starts after `swap()` resolves, so slow scene builds never eat the 0.9 s.
