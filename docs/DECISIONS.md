- 2026-11-08 — render: Reveal history is CPU-authoritative (Float32 master + RG8 DataTexture upload at 20 Hz) instead of a GPU-only RT. `revealAt` samples the exact bytes the shader samples, so gameplay visibility and pixels agree with no readback; the e2e probe measures max |GPU−CPU| field error ≈ 0.0014 with 0 visibility mismatches over 1024 points. - 2026-11-08 — render: Reveal noise comes from one tileable 256² RGBA8 value-noise texture (4 channels: reveal edge, band wobble, stroke breaks, grain). Cheaper than ALU fbm on mobile and mirrorable on the CPU (bilinear REPEAT, texel-centre exact). All reveal sources combine in *field* space with one shared paper-absorbency offset, so overlapping circles, trails and history merge with one coherent ragged edge.
- 2026-11-08 — render: Outline pick = **AB hybrid** (default). A alone loses environment silhouettes; B alone gives thin, uneven character lines at game distance. Toggle with `render.setOutlineMode('A'|'B'|'AB')` / harness "lines" button. B runs at half res when `quality.halfResEdges` (mobile auto, low).
